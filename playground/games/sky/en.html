Broken Sky OPEN BETA

The great wind has stopped. The bridges between the sky islands have snapped, and the islands are slowly sinking into the sea of clouds. You are a Mender, who joins broken paths again. A free isometric loot action RPG that runs right in your browser, no install, no sign-up.

PC: keyboard + mouse · Phone: turn sideways for touch controls

🧪 This is an open beta made by a tiny indie team. Use “💬 Send beta feedback” in the menu (≡), or the button at the end of each chapter. Your chapter and level are sent along, which helps a lot. Every piece of feedback is read.

What's inside

  • 5 classes: the spear-and-shield Shieldkeeper who shoves foes off island edges, the twin-blade Wandering Blade, the wind-and-lightning Windcaller, the crossbow-and-trap Trapper, and the Bellringer who fights with shockwaves and echo clones.
  • Branching skills: 6 skills per class in 3 branches (2 per branch), plus an ultimate from Lv 6. At skill level 5 and again at 10, each skill forks: you pick one of two upgrades that changes how it works. Skills in the same branch boost each other.
  • 4 stats: STR, AGI, VIT and BRE. You get 5 points to spend every level.
  • 14 gear slots, five rarities, unique items with their own stories, sets, blessed and cursed gear, identifying, and hidden options that unlock as you fight.
  • Enchanting up to +10 at the forge, with big success and failure moments. Weapons are safe to +6, armor to +4.
  • 7 chapters and an ending: Dandelion Isle → Rusty Windmill Isles → Anchored Harbor → Upside-down Forest → Thunder Nest → The Sinking Old Capital → First Wind Hill.
  • After the ending: harder difficulty tiers that unlock one by one, plus Drifting Isles opened with Sky Charts.
  • Daily stuff (optional, no penalty for missing days): village board requests, today's island chest, a rotating peddler, airship errands.

Controls

  • PC: left-click to move, right-click to attack, 1–4 skills (aimed at the mouse), Space to glide/dodge, Q/R potions, F talk, I bag, C character, S skills, M map, Esc pause.
  • Phone: turn it sideways. Drag on the left to move, big button on the right to attack, the buttons around it for skills.
